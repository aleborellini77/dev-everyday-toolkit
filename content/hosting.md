- [Down For Everyone Or Just Me](https://downforeveryoneorjustme.com): Rule out "is it just me" in five seconds.
- [Ping.eu](https://ping.eu): Ping and traceroute from multiple global locations.
- [IsItDownRightNow](https://www.isitdownrightnow.com): Cross-check outage reports for a specific site.
- [UptimeRobot Status](https://uptimerobot.com): Check the public status page a client already trusts.
- [Site24x7 Tools](https://www.site24x7.com/tools/): Free suite: ping, traceroute, port check, in one place.
- [IPinfo](https://ipinfo.io): Look up the ASN and hosting provider behind an IP.
